# Permanent project scope

Talent Forge is a single-player, World of Warcraft-inspired combat simulator for creating, configuring, playing, testing, and balancing custom classes.

- Keep exactly two gameplay entities: one fully player-controlled hero and one training dummy. The dummy is the only combat target.
- Keep the existing training environment and its current assets. Exclude NPCs, additional enemies, quests, dungeons, raids, PvP, multiplayer, party systems, new gameplay locations, world expansion, and exploration mechanics from future work.
- Focus development on hero controls and animations, combat and autoattack, custom abilities and spells, talents, character stats, equipment and weapons, buffs and debuffs, damage and healing, resources, cooldowns, combat UI and analytics, and class creation, testing, and balance.
- Make each new ability, talent, item, and combat mechanic testable in the existing arena with the hero and the same dummy. Self-targeted effects can apply to the hero during those tests.
